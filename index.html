Our World

Tradition, thoughtfully collected. Beauty, chosen to last.

Heritage Oasis presents a curated collection inspired by the rich artistic heritage of the East, the mastery of traditional craftsmanship and the timeless allure of natural materials.

From gemstone jewellery and handwoven carpets to fine Kashmiri textiles, artisanal décor and concentrated oil perfumes, every collection offers a distinctive expression of beauty, culture and individuality.

Heritage woven into every thread

Carpets & Textiles

Discover richly patterned carpets and exquisite textiles inspired by generations of traditional artistry.

Softness, detail & tradition

The Art of Kashmir

Experience the refined beauty of Kashmiri craftsmanship through luxurious cashmere and carefully selected textiles.

Celebrated for their softness, warmth and delicate artistry, these pieces reflect a long-standing tradition of textile excellence. From elegant wraps and scarves to distinctive embroidered fabrics, each creation brings understated sophistication to everyday living.

Objects with a story

Traditional Handicrafts

Our handicraft collection celebrates the skill of artisans and the beauty of traditional decorative arts.

Discover ornamental figures, handcrafted objects, intricate inlay work, decorative boxes and distinctive treasures inspired by regional traditions. Designed to be displayed, collected or gifted, these pieces introduce cultural character and artistic detail into any space.

Intimate, expressive and memorable, these fragrances offer a more personal approach to scent. Discover captivating compositions, elegant perfume oils and distinctive aromas selected for those who appreciate fragrance as an essential part of personal style.
